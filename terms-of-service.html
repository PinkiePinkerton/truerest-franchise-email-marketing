05. The 2-Minute Monthly Personalization Workflow

To achieve our benchmark 25% to 35% open rates, our emails avoid generic corporate blasts in favor of an authentic local voice:

  • Step 1 (We Draft): Each month, TSMC writes a complete email draft, constructs the layout, and configures all local booking and promo links.
  • Step 2 (You Provide Voice): We send you a preview along with a simple engagement prompt for you to write a one or two sentence story or thought about the monthly topic.
  • Step 3 (You Approve): You reply with your quick lines, give a thumbs-up, and TSMC drops your text into the opening hook and executes the deployment.

If a location fails to respond to a monthly prompt within 5 business days of scheduled distribution, TSMC will deploy an approved, high-converting location newsletter to ensure your members hear from you and your pods stay booked.
